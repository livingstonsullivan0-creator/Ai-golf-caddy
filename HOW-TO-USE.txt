============================================
   AI GOLF CADDY - HOW TO USE (SUPER EASY!)
============================================

NO CODING REQUIRED - JUST OPEN THE FILE!

STEP 1: Find the file
---------------------
Look for a file called: ai-golf-caddy.html

STEP 2: Open it
---------------
Double-click on "ai-golf-caddy.html"

That's it! The app will open in your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, etc.)


WHAT YOU'LL SEE:
================

The app has 5 tabs at the top:

1. RECOMMEND 🎯
   - Enter how far you need to hit
   - Set wind, temperature, and lie conditions
   - Click "Get Recommendations"
   - The app tells you which club to use!

2. LOG SHOT ➕
   - After you hit a shot, record it here
   - Pick your club, enter the distance
   - Add weather conditions
   - This helps the app learn YOUR game

3. SCORECARD 🏆
   - Choose a Denver golf course from the dropdown
   - Or use the Generic Course for any course
   - Enter your score for each hole
   - See if you're over/under par

4. STATS 📊
   - See your accuracy with each club
   - Find out which clubs you hit best
   - View average distances

5. HISTORY 📜
   - See all the shots you've logged
   - Review past performance


IMPORTANT NOTES:
================

✅ All your data is saved automatically in your browser
✅ Works offline after the first load
✅ No account needed, no sign-up
✅ Completely free
✅ Works on phones, tablets, and computers

❌ Don't delete your browser data or you'll lose your stats
❌ Data is only on YOUR device (not shared anywhere)


TROUBLESHOOTING:
================

Q: The file won't open!
A: Make sure it ends in .html - Try right-clicking it and choosing "Open With" → Your Web Browser

Q: I see code instead of the app!
A: You opened it in a text editor. Right-click → Open With → Chrome (or your browser)

Q: My data disappeared!
A: Did you clear your browser cache/cookies? That deletes the data. It's stored locally.

Q: Can I use this on my phone?
A: Yes! Transfer the ai-golf-caddy.html file to your phone and open it in your mobile browser.
